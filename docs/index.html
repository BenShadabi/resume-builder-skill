Resume Builder skill

From nothing to a resume, cover letter and interview prep that are honest, one page and matched to the job. You decide. Claude advises.

Pick your path

Quick

8 questions, 5 to 10 minutes. Paste your old resume and a posting.

Standard (recommended)

About 10 questions, 10 to 15 minutes. Most people.

Deep

Add-on rounds for academics, managers, career changers.

The 8 phases (click one, or watch it play)

Who decides what

You decide

  • Which documents you want
  • Roles, industries, locations
  • Length, order, tone, design
  • What to include or leave off
  • Whether to apply

Claude will not do

  • Invent or inflate a number, title or skill
  • Stuff keywords or hide AI use
  • Submit or sign in for you
  • Share your data

If a request goes against the process, Claude says why once, offers a better option, then follows your choice (except for the list above).

Send your files, Claude uses only those

Old resume, LinkedIn text, transcripts, project write-ups, reviews with numbers, job postings. Claude does not search the web or invent "typical" duties. Everything comes from you, and each fact is tagged with its source. No passwords, ID or bank details.

What you need

  • 5 to 15 minutes, and honest numbers from your work if you have them
  • Files to send (old resume, LinkedIn text, anything with real numbers) and 1 to 3 job postings, all optional
  • Python 3 and Chrome, Chromium or Edge. Nothing else to install.
