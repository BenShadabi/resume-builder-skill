# Pick a path

People are busy and most will not answer a long questionnaire. Offer these three paths at the start, recommend one, and let the person choose. They can switch later. Honest trade-off: fewer questions means less detail to work with, so the Quick path gives a solid but plainer resume, and the Deep path gives the most complete one.

| Path | Questions | Time | Best for | You get |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Quick** | 8 (the (Q) ones) | about 5 to 10 minutes | In a hurry, has an old resume to paste, one job in mind | A tailored one-page resume and a "needs your input" list |
| **Standard** (default) | up to 11 (usually 8 to 10 asked) | about 10 to 15 minutes | Most people | Master resume, tailored resumes, plus a cover letter and prep sheet if wanted |
| **Deep** | 11 plus the add-on rounds | 40+ minutes | Academics, managers, career changers, many jobs, complex stories | The fullest fact sheet and the strongest tailoring |

## How to offer it
Say it in two lines: "I can do this three ways: Quick (8 questions, 5 to 10 minutes, best if you can paste your old resume), Standard (about 10 questions, about 10 to 15 minutes, what I recommend), or Deep (more detail for complicated situations). Which do you want?" Then follow their pick.

## Quick path rules
- Start with Q3: ask for files (old resume, LinkedIn text, anything with real numbers) and the posting. Pull facts from them into `profile.md`, mark everything you inferred as `CONFIRM`, and ask only the Q questions that are still unanswered.
- Still run the date sanity check before drafting (for example an expected graduation date that falls before the target term, or overlapping dates).
- If the resume would fill under about two thirds of a page, ask the one question that helps most: "What is one thing you did that a manager praised or you did often, and how was it counted?"
- Mark skipped topics "not asked" in `profile.md` so they are never assumed.
- Output: the master resume and one tailored resume, `needs-input.md`, and a prep sheet only if they have an interview soon.
- Offer to ask the rest later ("want me to do the 12 other questions now or another day?").

## What never gets skipped on any path
The read-back (Phase 2), the hard rules, the "Never write" list and the checks. Short does not mean unchecked.

## If they stop mid-way
Save what you have in `profile.md`, say how many questions are left, and offer to build from what you have now.
