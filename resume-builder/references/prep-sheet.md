# Interview prep sheet

One per posting, only if the person wants it. A prep sheet is for the person, not for the employer, so it is plain notes in `prep.md` (no PDF needed unless they ask).

## Sections
1. **30-second intro:** who you are, one proof point, why this role.
2. **Company in 60 seconds:** what it does, size or reach, and where the facts came from.
3. **Three recent items:** projects, deals, launches or news with dates. Hedge anything unconfirmed.
4. **The role in plain words:** what you would do in week one.
5. **Resume bullets, one by one:** for each bullet a likely follow-up question and a 3-line story: what it was, what I did, what changed. Use only facts in `profile.md`. If a story detail is missing, write "Ask me" and let the person fill it in their words.
6. **AI process:** for each AI-assisted project, the step AI did, what the person did and how they checked it, so they can explain it.
7. **Other likely questions:** why this company, strengths, a weakness, a failure, a conflict, a time you used data.
8. **Technical check:** what the posting suggests they may be tested on, and what the person should practise.
9. **Words to know:** terms from the posting they should be able to explain.
10. **Questions to ask them:** 3 to 5 specific ones.
11. **Needs your input:** eligibility questions, missing facts, deadline, assessments or video interviews, and anything they must do themselves.
12. **Sources.**

## Rules
- Never write a story the person has not told you. The point is to help them remember their own experience.
- Keep each bullet's answer short enough to say in about 30 seconds.
- Flag skills at "learning" level so they are ready to be honest about them.
