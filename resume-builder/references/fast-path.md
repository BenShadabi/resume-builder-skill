# Fast path (interview this week, deadline soon)

Use when the person says they are in a hurry. It is their choice; offer it, explain what is skipped, then follow what they pick.

## What you keep (about 12 to 15 questions)
1. Round 1 basics: Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4 (situation), Q5, Q8 (work authorization flag).
2. Round 2: Q9 (target titles) and Q10 (one or two industries).
3. Round 3: Q17, Q18 (degree, school, dates).
4. Round 4: for the 2 most relevant jobs only, Q24, Q27, Q28 (tasks, results, numbers).
5. Round 5: the 1 or 2 best projects, Q33 to Q37.
6. Round 6: Q39 (tools and honest levels) and Q41 (languages).
7. Round 7: Q46 (AI use and what they did themselves).
8. Paste the one posting that matters.

## What you skip
Rounds 2 (rest), 3 (courses, awards), 7 (clubs and extras), 8, extra jobs and the cover letter unless they ask.

## What never gets skipped
The read-back (Phase 2), the hard rules, and the checks. Fast does not mean unchecked. Mark anything skipped as "not asked" in `profile.md` so it is never assumed.

## Output
A master resume and one tailored resume, a prep sheet if they have an interview, and `needs-input.md`. Offer to run the rest of the interview later.
